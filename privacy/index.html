PRIVACY POLICY

prodbygrillo · Last updated: June 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how prodbygrillo ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the prodbygrillo content management dashboard ("the Service"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of users who connect their TikTok accounts to this Service.

2. Information We Collect

When you authorise the Service through TikTok Login, we may access the following information as permitted by the scopes you grant:

  • Basic profile information (display name, profile link)
  • Account statistics (follower count, likes count, video count)
  • List of your public TikTok videos

We do not collect or store your TikTok password. Authentication is handled entirely through TikTok's official OAuth 2.0 flow.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through TikTok's API is used exclusively to:

  • Display your account information within the private dashboard
  • Upload video content to your TikTok account as drafts on your behalf
  • Show your TikTok video history within the dashboard

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

4. Data Storage

OAuth access tokens are stored locally on the device running the dashboard and are used solely to authenticate API requests on your behalf. No personal data is transmitted to external servers other than TikTok's official API endpoints.

5. TikTok API Data Use

Data obtained through TikTok's API is used only for the purposes described in this policy and in accordance with TikTok's Privacy Policy and TikTok API Developer Terms of Service.

6. Data Retention

Access tokens are retained only as long as necessary to provide the Service. You can revoke access at any time through your TikTok account settings under "Connected Apps".

7. Your Rights

You have the right to revoke this application's access to your TikTok account at any time. To do so, visit TikTok Settings → Privacy → Connected Apps and remove prodbygrillo.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us via beatstars.com/prodbygrillo.
